## Overview

A cycle is a fixed period of time, and some teams call it a sprint. A cycle
helps a team to give its attention to one part of the work. The cycle view of
the team shows the issues in the active cycle. The view also shows the progress
of the team, because each issue moves through the workflow.

![](/img/docs/cycles.png)

## How to enable cycles

Each team enables cycles for itself:

1. Open **Settings → your team → Overview**.
2. Under **Team preferences**, enable cycles.

A **Cycles** entry then appears in the sidebar of the team, and each issue gets
a cycle property.

## How to use cycles

- **Assign an issue.** Set the cycle in the properties panel at the right of
  the issue. You can also set the cycle for many issues together, from the list
  view or the board view.
- **See the progress.** The view of the active cycle groups the issues by
  status, so you see the work that is not complete.
- **Move between cycles.** A team with the automatic cadence needs no manual
  step. A scheduled job in the server completes each cycle after its end date.
  The job then moves the unfinished issues, as the preference of the team tells
  it to, and it makes more future cycles. A team that controls its cycles
  manually keeps full control, and the job never changes it. Read
  [Scheduled jobs](/docs/oss/self-deployment#scheduled-jobs).
