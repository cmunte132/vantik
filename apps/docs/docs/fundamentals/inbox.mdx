## Overview

The inbox shows the issues that need your attention. Click **Inbox** in the
sidebar to open it. The badge on **Inbox** shows the number of issues with an
unread notification.

![The inbox. The list at the left shows two issues. The open issue at the right
shows a new comment and a box for a reply.](/img/docs/issues/inbox.png)

The inbox shows one row for each issue, the newest first. Each row shows:

- The title and the key of the issue. The title of an unread row is darker.
- What happened, and who did it. For example: "Assigned by grace" or "New
  comment from grace".
- "+N more" if the issue has more notifications.
- The time of the last change to the issue.

## How to read a notification

1. Click a row. Vantik marks all the notifications of that issue as read.
2. Read the updates at the right. The oldest update is first.
3. To reply to a comment, type in the comment box at the bottom.
4. To open the full issue, click **Open issue**.

A comment shows in full. A different update shows as one line, for example
"moved this to Done" or "set the priority to Urgent".

You cannot mark a row as unread, delete it, or snooze it.

## What sends a notification

Vantik sends a notification only for these events:

| Event | Who gets it |
| --- | --- |
| A person assigns the issue to you. | You. |
| A person takes the issue off you. | You. |
| A person comments on the issue. | The subscribers of the issue. |
| A person moves the issue to a completed status. | The subscribers of the issue. |
| A person sets the priority of the issue to **Urgent**. | The subscribers of the issue. |
| A person adds a **Blocks** or a **Blocked by** relation to the issue. | The subscribers of the issue. |

Vantik does not send a notification for a change that you make yourself. It
does not send one for other changes, for example a new label or a change to a
status that is not completed.

One change to an issue sends a maximum of one notification. If a change does
more than one of these things, the assignment wins. Then the status, and then
the priority.

If you already have a notification for an issue, a change of status or of
priority does not add a second notification.

When a person deletes an issue, Vantik removes its notifications.

## Subscriptions

The subscribers of an issue get the notifications of that issue. Vantik
subscribes these people automatically:

- The person who creates the issue, and its assignee.
- Each person who changes the issue, and each new assignee.
- Each person who comments on the issue.
- Each person that a comment mentions with `@`.

To subscribe or unsubscribe by hand, open the issue. Next to the **Comments**
and **Activity** tabs, click **Subscribe** or **Unsubscribe**. The avatars next
to the button show the subscribers.

When you change an issue again, Vantik subscribes you again.

## Notification settings

You choose which notifications you get, and where you get them.

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Under **My account**, click **Notifications**.
3. For each type of notification, turn on or turn off **Inbox** and **Email**.

![The Notifications settings. Each row has an Inbox switch and an Email
switch.](/img/docs/settings/notifications.png)

The types are **Assignments**, **Comments**, **Completed**, **Marked urgent**
and **Blocked**. The **Everything** row changes all the types at the same
time. All the types are on by default. Vantik saves each change immediately.

Vantik sends email through the SMTP server of your instance. If your instance
has no SMTP server, Vantik sends no email, but the inbox still works.

## The inbox and Needs you

The inbox and **Needs you** are two different lists.

- **The inbox** is personal. It shows the notifications about issues.
- **Needs you** is for all members of the workspace. It shows the decisions
  about the knowledge bank that wait for a person. To open it, click **Pages**
  in the sidebar, and then click **Needs you**. The badge on **Pages** shows
  the number of open items.

Read [Knowledge](/fundamentals/knowledge) for more about Needs you.
