## Overview

Two pages show the issues of more than one team:

- **My issues** shows the issues that are assigned to you.
- **All issues** shows all the issues of your teams.

The **Issues** page of a team shows only the issues of that team. Read
[Issues](/fundamentals/issues).

## My issues

Click **My issues** in the sidebar.

![The My issues page. It shows the issues assigned to the user, grouped by
status.](/img/docs/issues/my-issues.png)

The page shows the issues that are assigned to you, in all your teams. It has
no tabs.

If you add an **Assignee** filter, the filter replaces this rule. The page then
shows the issues of the assignees that you select. Remove the filter to see
your issues again.

## All issues

The **All issues** page of the workspace shows each issue in your teams. The
sidebar has no link to it. Use one of these methods to open it:

- Go to `/<workspace>/all` in the browser. Replace `<workspace>` with the slug
  of your workspace.
- On the page of a module, click the link that tells how many issues touch the
  module. The page opens with a **Module** filter for that module.

## The controls of these pages

Both pages have the same controls as the **Issues** page of a team:

- **Filter**. Read [Views](/fundamentals/views#how-to-filter-the-issues).
- The layout, **Group by**, **Order by** and **Display options**. Read
  [How to group, order and show the issues](/fundamentals/issues#how-to-group-order-and-show-the-issues).
- **Save as view**, when the page has filters. A view that you save from these
  pages belongs to the workspace.

On **All issues**, `⌘ K` and `F` work. `S`, `A`, `L` and `P` do not work
there, because the page has no team. No issue shortcut works on **My issues**.
Read [Keyboard shortcuts](/fundamentals/shortcuts).
