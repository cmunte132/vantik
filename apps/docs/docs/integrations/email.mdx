## Overview

Send an email to a team's address. The Email integration makes a triage issue
in that team, with the email thread in the description and its attachments on
the issue. A reply in the same thread goes onto the same issue.

## How to configure the integration

1. Open **Settings → Integrations → Email** and connect the mailbox.
2. Under **Team mappings**, pair an address tag with a team — for example
   `support` with your Support team.
3. Mail sent to `you+workspace-support@…` now lands in triage of that team,
   where `workspace` is this workspace's slug and `you@…` is the mailbox you
   connected.

Mail whose tag has no pair is left alone.
