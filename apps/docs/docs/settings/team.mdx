Each team has its own settings. To open them, open **Settings**. In the
settings sidebar, open the team. Then click **Overview**, **Members**,
**Templates**, **Workflow**, **Cycles** or **Labels**.

## Overview

The **Team** section holds the **Team name** and the **Team identifier**.

1. Change the **Team name** or the **Team identifier**.
2. Click **Update**.

The name must have 2 to 50 characters. The identifier must have 2 to 5
characters. Each member of the team can change them. A workspace admin can
also change them.

> **Warning:** Do not change the identifier if people use the issue keys of the
> team. The key of each issue of the team changes with the identifier. Read
> [Team identifiers and issue keys](/concepts#team-identifiers-and-issue-keys).

![The Overview settings of a team, with the Team name and Team identifier
fields](/img/docs/settings/team-overview.png)

### Create by email

The **Team preferences** section shows an email address for the team, with a
copy button. The address ends in `@vantik.dev`. No part of Vantik reads mail
at this address, so an email to it does not make an issue.

To make triage issues from email, use the Email integration. Read
[Email](/integrations/email).

### How to delete a team

Only a workspace admin can delete a team. You can delete a team only when it
has no issues.

1. In **Danger zone**, click **Delete this team**.
2. Click **Yes, delete team**.

If the team has issues, Vantik tells you how many. Move or delete them first.

## Members

The **Team Members** section lists the members of the team.

![The Team Members settings, with the list of members of the
team](/img/docs/settings/team-members.png)

Only a workspace admin can add or remove a member of a team.

To add a member:

1. Click **Add member**.
2. Select a member of the workspace. The list shows only the members that are
   not in the team.

To add a person who is not in the workspace, click **Invite people**. Read
[How to invite a person](/settings/workspace#how-to-invite-a-person).

To remove a member, click **...** on the row of the member, and select
**Remove from team**.

## Templates

The **Templates** section holds the issue templates of the team. Read
[Templates](/fundamentals/templates).

![The Templates settings of a team, with two
templates](/img/docs/settings/templates.png)

## Workflow

The **Workflow** section holds the states that an issue of the team can have.
Each state belongs to one category: **Triage**, **Backlog**, **Unstarted**,
**Started**, **Completed** or **Canceled**. The category tells Vantik what a
state means, for example that an issue is done.

![The Workflow settings of a team, with the states in each
category](/img/docs/settings/workflow.png)

To add a state:

1. Click **+** next to a category.
2. Select a color, and type the **Workflow name**.
3. Optional: type a **Workflow description**.
4. Click **Save**.

To change a state, put the pointer on it, and click the pencil. To change the
order of the states, drag a state. A state stays in its category. You cannot
delete a state.

The **Triage** category holds the issues that a person must sort. Read
[Triage](/fundamentals/triage).

## Cycles

The **Cycles** section turns cycles on for the team, and sets how Vantik makes
them. Read [Cycles](/fundamentals/cycles).

![The Cycles settings of a team, with cycles
enabled](/img/docs/settings/cycles.png)

## Labels

The **Team labels** section holds the labels that only this team can use. It
works as the workspace labels do: click **New label**, type the **Label name**,
and click **Save**. Click **...** on a row to edit or delete a label.

The labels of the workspace do not show here. Read
[Workspace settings](/settings/workspace#labels).

![The Team labels settings, with the labels of the
team](/img/docs/settings/team-labels.png)
