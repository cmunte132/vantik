The workspace settings apply to all members of the workspace. To open them,
open **Settings**. Then click an item under **Workspace** in the settings
sidebar: **Overview**, **Labels**, **Members**, **Agents**, **Integrations** or
**Export**.

Each member can see each of these pages. Some actions are for admins only. Read
[Roles](/concepts#roles). The **Agents** page has its own docs page:
[Agent settings](/settings/agents).

## Overview

The **Profile** section holds the **Workspace name**.

1. Change the **Workspace name**.
2. Click **Update**.

You cannot change the URL of the workspace. Vantik makes it from the first name
of the workspace, when you make the workspace.

![The Overview settings of the workspace, with the Workspace name field and the
Danger zone section](/img/docs/settings/workspace-overview.png)

The **Danger zone** section shows a **Delete this workspace** button. The
button does nothing now. Vantik has no way to delete a workspace from the
webapp.

## Labels

The **Workspace labels** section holds the labels that all teams can use. To
make a label that only one team can use, open the **Labels** settings of that
team. Read [Team settings](/settings/team#labels).

![The Workspace labels settings, with a list of
labels](/img/docs/settings/labels.png)

To make a label:

1. Click **New label**.
2. Type the **Label name**.
3. Click **Save**.

Vantik gives the new label a color. You cannot select the color.

To change or delete a label, click **...** on its row. Then select **Edit** or
**Delete**. **Edit** changes the name only. **Delete** asks you to confirm.
Click **Continue** to delete the label.

Use **Filter by name** to find a label. Each member can make, change and delete
a workspace label.

## Members

The **Members** section lists the members of the workspace, with the role of
each member. The list also shows the agents of the workspace, with the role
`AGENT`. Read [Roles](/concepts#roles). A member that an admin suspended shows under **Suspended**. Use
**Filter by name** to find a member by name or by email.

![The Members settings, with the list of members and their
roles](/img/docs/settings/members.png)

### How to invite a person

Only an admin can invite a person. The **Add member** button shows for each
member, but the invite fails if you are not an admin.

1. Click **Add member**.
2. In **Emails**, type one or more email addresses. Put a comma between two
   addresses.
3. In **Invite as**, select **User** or **Admin**. The default is **User**.
4. In **Add to teams**, select one or more teams. You must select a team.
5. Click **Invite**.

Vantik sends an email to each address. The email has a link that signs the
person in. The link works for 7 days.

When the person signs in, Vantik shows the invites of that person. The person
clicks **Accept** or **Decline**. If the person accepts, Vantik adds the person
to the workspace with the role and the teams of the invite.

To send the email again, invite the same address again. This does not change
the role or the teams of the invite. You cannot cancel an invite.

### How to suspend a member

Only an admin sees the menu on the row of a member.

1. Click **...** on the row of the member.
2. Select **Suspend**.

The webapp does not let a suspended member open the workspace.

Vantik also refuses most requests from the access tokens of a suspended
member. Some actions do not check the suspension, for example the CSV export.

> **Warning:** Do not rely on a suspension to stop the personal agents of a
> member. Each agent has its own membership, and a suspension does not change
> it. To stop these agents, revoke them in
> [Agent accounts](/settings/agents#agent-accounts).

The webapp has no way to undo a suspension. It also has no way to change the role of a member, or to remove a
member from the workspace.

To add a member to a team or to remove a member from a team, use the
**Members** settings of the team. Read [Team settings](/settings/team#members).

## Integrations

The **Integrations** section shows each integration that the workspace can
use. Click **View** on an integration to set it up. An integration that needs
credentials shows **Needs credentials**.

![The Integrations settings, with a card for each
integration](/img/docs/settings/integrations.png)

Read [Integrations](/integrations/overview).

## Export

The **Export** section makes a CSV file of your issues.

1. Click **Export CSV**.
2. Your browser downloads the file `issues.csv`.

The file holds the issues of the teams that you are a member of. It does not
hold deleted issues. Each row is one issue, with these columns:

- Number, Title and Description. The description is plain text.
- State, Labels and Priority. The priority is a number.
- Parent Issue.
- Created At and Updated At.
- Created By. This is the ID of the user, not the name.
- Team and Assignee.

The export holds issues only. It does not hold comments, projects, cycles or
pages. Each member can export.

![The Export settings, with the Export CSV
button](/img/docs/settings/export.png)

## What a new workspace starts with

When you make an account, Vantik asks for your **Full name**, a **Workspace
name**, a **Team name** and a **Team identifier**. Then click **Continue**.

![The onboarding form for a new account and its
workspace](/img/docs/getting-started/onboarding.png)

Vantik then makes these things:

- The workspace. You are its first admin.
- One team, with the name and the identifier that you gave. You are a member
  of it.
- The workflow states of the team: **Triage**, **Unscoped**, **Backlog**,
  **Todo**, **In Progress**, **In Review**, **Done** and **Canceled**.
- Six workspace labels: **Bug**, **Feature**, **Design**, **Documentation**,
  **Frontend** and **Backend**.

If a person invited you to a workspace, Vantik shows the invites, not this
form.
